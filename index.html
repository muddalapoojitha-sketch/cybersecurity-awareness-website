Common Cyber Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, websites, or messages designed to steal passwords and personal information.

🦠 Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices, spy on users, or steal sensitive data.

🔐 Weak Passwords

Simple or reused passwords make it easier for attackers to access your accounts.

📶 Unsafe Wi‑Fi

Public Wi‑Fi networks can expose your data if they are insecure or controlled by attackers.

📱 Social Media Safety

Protect your personal information while using social media.

  • Keep your account privacy settings secure.
  • Don't share personal information publicly.
  • Be careful when accepting unknown requests.
  • Report suspicious accounts or messages.

📧 Email Safety

Stay alert when reading emails and opening attachments.

  • Check the sender before replying.
  • Don't open unexpected attachments.
  • Be careful with urgent requests.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs through email.

🌐 Safe Browsing

Browse the internet carefully and protect your personal information.

  • Use trusted and secure websites.
  • Check for HTTPS before entering sensitive information.
  • Avoid downloading files from unknown websites.
  • Don't enter personal information on suspicious pages.

Phishing Awareness

Phishing is a fake email, message, or website used to steal your personal information.

Always check the sender's email address before clicking links.

Never share passwords, OTPs, or bank details through unknown messages.

Understanding Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack that uses disguised emails or websites to trick you into providing sensitive information. Always verify the sender's email address, look for spelling errors, and avoid clicking on suspicious links.

Cybersecurity Safety Tips

  • Use long, unique passwords for every account.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) whenever possible.
  • Do not click suspicious links or download unknown attachments.
  • Keep your phone, computer, browser, and apps updated.
  • Check website URLs carefully before entering personal details.
  • Avoid accessing sensitive accounts on public Wi‑Fi.
